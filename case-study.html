The problem

Two agents used AI. Four didn't trust it.

Two agents used ChatGPT for listings; the rest had tried it once and gotten generic copy. Client updates went out late in busy weeks, and every listing sounded different depending on who wrote it.

What we did

Three sessions, one shared setup

  1. Intake: each agent answered five questions about their week and how they used AI.
  2. Session 1: the C.A.F.E. brief on real listings, then a shared team Project with brand voice, example listings and fair housing rules.
  3. Session 2: Gmail and Calendar connected; weekly client updates drafted from rough notes.
  4. Session 3: each agent built a weekday morning brief as a scheduled task.
  5. Day 7 check-in: what stuck, what didn't, and one fix.

What they can do now

Same quality from every agent

Questions about the setups

Anything else, ask in your free audit.

Do the setups need tech skills?

No. If you can write an email, you can run them.

Do the setups need new software?

Usually not. Some need a paid Claude or ChatGPT plan; we check yours in the audit.

Do the setups follow fair housing rules?

When a licensed agent reviews it first, yes. Every setup describes the property, never the buyer.

Do the setups keep working without you?

Yes. Everything lives in your own accounts, and the tune-ups are optional.

Are setup sessions in person or on Zoom?

Both. In person across Raleigh, Durham, Cary and Chapel Hill, and on Zoom anywhere.

Questions agents ask

Do I need to be good with tech?

No. If you can write an email, you can do this. We go at your pace.

Do I have to buy new software?

Usually not. We use Claude or ChatGPT plus the Google and Canva tools you likely have. Connected setups like the Daily Agent Brief usually need a paid AI plan, and we check what you have during the audit.

Is AI-written listing copy OK under fair housing rules?

Yes, if a licensed agent reviews it before it goes out. Every setup describes the property, never the buyer, and includes a fair housing check. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

What about client privacy?

Training sessions use sample data, and nothing goes out without your review. You leave with clear rules for what never goes into AI tools.

Will I end up depending on you?

No. Everything lives in your own accounts, and you learn how each setup works. Ongoing tune-ups are optional.

My brokerage already offers AI classes.

That’s a good place to start. This is the next step: set up in your own accounts, on your real work, with someone to call afterward.
